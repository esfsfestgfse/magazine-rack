## Duplicate and quality findings

The sampled raw first pages exposed duplicates before UI grouping: Magazines 4, EU Comics 18, TV Guide 3, Pop Mechanics 2, and several smaller shelves 1 each. Cross-shelf grouping labels the overlap instead of silently presenting it as a unique issue. The release retains the existing title/identifier deduplication and issue metadata grouping.

## Demotions and decisions

- Europeana, EU Comics, and XKCD remain visible but explicitly marked `SECONDARY SOURCE` and `Image only` because they do not provide a reliable in-app document reader.
- Comic Book Plus remains primary because the final health pass returned readable in-app issues; its source status is independently visible.
- No primary shelf was hidden: each returned populated, readable content in the settled audit.
- Open Library was narrowed so the general rack no longer acts as a broad comics/books catch-all; OL Comics remains the dedicated comic catalog.
- Manga classification now rejects known false-positive phrases such as “How to Draw Manga,” art/tutorial records, stage works, and unrelated titles while keeping actual manga volumes and series.
