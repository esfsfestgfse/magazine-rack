# Magazine Rack live shelf audit — 2026-09-23

Audited the live root at `https://esfsfestgfse.github.io/magazine-rack/index.html` in a real browser. The page rendered 52 live shelves; the primary racks were populated and the restricted racks were last in the shelf order.

## Findings

| Shelf | Live availability | Reader state | Finding |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Magazines | 583,446 | Read in app | Mixed comics, official bulletins, scientific backfiles, and administrative records were leaking into the general rack. |
| Gaming | 41,967 | Read in app | False positives included `Dippy`, `Graustark`, `House of Lords`, `Lone Star Diplomat`, and `Diplophobia`. |
| Horror | 2,247 | Read in app / borrow | Horror issues were mixed with books such as `Bible Now!`, `Fear nothing`, `Silent Scream`, and unrelated titles. |
| Adventure | 1,160 | Read in app / borrow | Argosy books, a casino legal record, an expedition report, and table-of-contents records were present. |
| Open Library | 43,986 | Borrow / preview | Catalog and subject-book noise included cookbooks, directories, literary-periodical studies, and generic `Magazines` records. |
| Manga | 101,505 | Borrow / preview | Populated with identifiable manga and graphic editions; no equivalent noise gate was required in this pass. |
| Papers | 284 | Read in app | Calendar-day filtering worked: visible issues matched September 23 across historical years. |
| Secondary feeds | varies | Image only / fallback | Europeana and XKCD remain explicitly secondary; they were not promoted into primary shelves. |
| Adult Mags | 22,561 | Read in app | Adult titles were correctly at the bottom and no longer occupied the top of the page. |

## Changes made

- Added shelf-specific relevance gates before cards are rendered.
- Removed obvious comic/admin leakage from the general Magazines rack.
- Required gaming titles to contain gaming anchors and rejected the observed false-positive families.
- Required Horror titles to match identifiable horror periodical anchors and rejected observed book/anthology noise.
- Required Adventure titles to match the periodical families and rejected Argosy books, legal records, expeditions, and table-of-contents records.
- Removed obvious catalog/subject-book noise from Open Library without removing the borrowable source.
- Kept provider totals as provider totals; visible cards remain the curated/readable subset.
